CLIMINVEST

AI for Climate Adaptation Project Investment Case Creation
October 2nd, 2026
School of Computing Science, University of Glasgow

Summary

CLIMINVEST (https://climinvest-horizon.eu/) is a European initiative bringing together sustainability experts to reshape how climate adaptation projects are funded, evaluated and implemented. Focusing on bankable-by-design solutions, the project equips green project owners, investors and public authorities with the tools they need to develop climate adaptation projects that are not only effective, but also financially viable. Through technical innovation, strategic alignment with ESG frameworks, and a robust stakeholder ecosystem, CLIMINVEST lowers the barriers to investment and accelerates the deployment of high impact projects across Europe. Artificial intelligence (AI) has a central role in this project, as it can assist related parties on aspects like extracting useful bits of information from existing documents, answering complex finance-related questions, or helping write investment cases that can be presented to financial institutions.

In this half-day event, we will have speakers from both academia and industry presenting key aspects and insights from the on-going CLIMINVEST project, with a focus on the AI technologies that CLIMINVEST uses to assist project owners on developing green infrastructure project concepts, and their integration within the CLIMINVEST Bankability-By-Design Framework: the main methodology designed by the project to drive a project from the earliest conceptual stages to a financially viable scheme. The event will combine talks from international experts in the field, along with technical demonstrations of the developed technologies. This event is recommended for both academic and industry practitioners who are interested in the challenges of climate financing, advances in the field of explainable and auditable AI assisted document creation, as well as frameworks and tools for analysing green investment projects.

This event is part of the Fintech Scotland Festival 2026.
